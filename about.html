CV – Emil Kjerrman

Emil Kjerrman (b. 1990, Copenhagen) is a visual artist based in Leipzig and Copenhagen. He primarily works with painting, combining self-produced pigments and oil paint with classical techniques in a contemporary pictorial practice. His work explores the tension between materiality and motif, the raw and the cultivated.

Kjerrman graduated from the Bergen Academy of Art and Design (2019–2022) and will finish his studies at the Hochschule für Grafik und Buchkunst in Leipzig in the summer 2026. He has exhibited in Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Germany, and China, in both solo presentations and major group exhibitions.
