About

Emil Kjerrman (b. 1990, Copenhagen) is a visual artist based in Leipzig. He primarily works with painting, combining self-produced pigments and oil paint with classical techniques in a contemporary pictorial practice. His work explores the tension between materiality and motif, the raw and the cultivated.

Kjerrman graduated from the Bergen Academy of Art and Design (2019–2022) and is currently studying at the Hochschule für Grafik und Buchkunst (HGB) in Leipzig (2023–2026). He has exhibited in Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Germany, and China, both in solo presentations and in major group exhibitions.

His awards include the Knud Højgaard Foundation grant for study abroad (2023) and Grosserer L. F. Foght’s grant for catalogue publication, "Zwischen uns und der Sonne hängen Ozeane aus leerem Raum". Upcoming exhibitions include De Blide at the Nordic Contemporary Art Center in Xiamen, China (2026).
