Emil Kjerrman

Visual Artist
Born 1990, Copenhagen, Denmark
Lives and works in Leipzig, Germany

Emil Kjerrman is a visual artist primarily working with painting. His practice combines self-produced pigments and oil paint with classical techniques within a contemporary pictorial framework. His work explores tensions between materiality and motif, the raw and the cultivated.

Kjerrman graduated from the Bergen Academy of Art and Design (2019–2022) and is currently studying at the Hochschule für Grafik und Buchkunst (HGB) in Leipzig (2023–2026). He has exhibited in Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Germany, and China.
